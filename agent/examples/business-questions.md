# Example Business Questions

OMEGA can be used to ask business questions in natural language.

## Sales Analysis

- What are the total sales?
- Show sales by month.
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- What are the major sales trends?

## Customer Analysis

- Which customers have the highest purchase value?
- Who are the top customers?
- Show customer-wise sales.

## Payment Analysis

- What are the pending payments?
- How many payments are completed?
- Show the payment status breakdown.

## Product Analysis

- Which products are selling the most?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Compare product performance.

## Business Insights

- What are the key trends in the business data?
- Which areas require attention?
- Summarize the important business insights from the available data.
